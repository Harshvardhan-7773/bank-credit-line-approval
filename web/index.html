Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Regulatory Framework

Governed by RBI Master Directions on Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct (2022/2026), Fair Practices Code (FPC), and Credit Information Companies (CIC) Act.

Mandatory Credit Rejection Disclosure

Under RBI Fair Practices Code, when a commercial bank or NBFC declines an application or extends a reduced credit line, it must convey the principal reasons in writing to the applicant based on objective underwriting criteria.

FOIR & Unsecured Exposure Norms

Prudential retail lending standards limit total monthly debt obligations (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio - FOIR) to a maximum of 50%-55% of verifiable net monthly earnings to prevent borrower debt distress.
